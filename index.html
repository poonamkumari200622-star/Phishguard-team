STAY INFORMED

Know the Common Scams

Learn to recognize common online fraud techniques.

🏦

Banking Fraud

Fake banking messages may ask you to verify your account details.

Tip: Never share your OTP or PIN.
🎁

Lottery Scams

Fraudsters may promise prizes in exchange for personal information or money.

Tip: Verify unexpected prize claims.
📦

Delivery Scams

Fake delivery links may ask for payment or personal information.

Tip: Check the official delivery website.
💼

Job Scams

Fake recruiters may demand registration fees or sensitive documents.

Tip: Verify the company before applying.
